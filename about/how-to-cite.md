---
title: How to cite this book
---

There are several ways to link to and cite this book. Every citation follows the citation style you choose in the settings (Aa). Under **Copy citations as**, the settings also let you choose how citations are copied:

- **Formatted** (the preset) keeps the citation's formatting: the title in italics, and the address and the in-text citation as links. Use it when you paste into a word processor such as Word, Pages or Google Docs, where the citation arrives ready to use.
- **Plain text** copies the words alone, without italics or links. Use it when you paste into an email, a notes app, a form or a Markdown editor. Some of these apps turn formatting into stray symbols, such as asterisks around the title, or keep a font and size that don't match your text.

Your choice is kept in your browser, as the text size is, and applies to every copy on the site.

# Cite a section

Each section starts with a short note at the top of its margin, or above the text on a phone. The note says who made the section and when. Its **Copy citation** button copies the citation for that section.

# Cite a quote

Select words in any section and a **Copy quote** button appears at the foot of the window. It copies the quote, its citation and a link to the paragraph it comes from. The citation is numbered by paragraph, for example (Nassar 2026: para. 55). If you copy in the usual way (Cmd/Ctrl+C), you get only the words, without the citation.

# Cite a picture, recording, video or document

Every picture, recording, video and document in the book is a numbered item. The number is at the start of its caption.

- **Click the number** to open the item's page. Under "To cite" it gives the item's citation, with a **Copy citation** button.
- **In the zoom viewer**, the **Cite** button at the top copies the citation of the picture or document you are viewing.

An item is cited as part of the book, with its own short address, for example (Puwar 2026: item 37) and hearhere.matteringpress.org/37/. Where the item's source has asked to be credited in a particular way, the citation includes that too.

# Cite a reference

Every work the book cites is listed in [References](/about/references-and-media/#references), which is part of References and media in About this book. Under each work are the passages that cite it.

- **In the text**, click a citation such as (Hall 2001) to see the full reference. Its **Copy citation** button copies it.
- **On the References page**, the **Copy citation** button beside each work copies its reference.
- **For a reference manager**, the top of the References page has the whole bibliography as **BibTeX**, **RIS** or **CSL-JSON**.

Like every other copy on the site, references follow the citation style and the formatted or plain-text setting you choose in Aa.

# Link to a paragraph

Every paragraph has a number in the left margin. Point at the number and a ¶ appears beside it. Click it to copy a link straight to that paragraph.

# Citations and versions

The book is published in versions. Each version that has been released stays online at its own address, for example hearhere.matteringpress.org/v0.1/, and its paragraph numbers never change. A citation always names a version, so it keeps leading to the text you read.

The book is also changed between versions. If you copy a quote from the latest text and its paragraphs are unchanged in the newest version, the citation points to that version and uses its paragraph numbers. If the passage is new or has changed since, the citation names the "latest text, unreleased" and tells you that the paragraph number may still change. All versions are listed under [Versions and kit](./colophon.md#versions-and-kit) in the Colophon.

# Citation style and reference managers

Citations follow Mattering Press' house style, which is based on MHRA. You can change to APA, Chicago, MLA or Harvard in the settings (Aa).
