**Time Table and Schedule – Walking, Sitting, Sketching**

9am – pick up booklets \[correct some\]: 10am- to cathedral: 10.15 – meet outside cathedral

**10.30 – Walk begins**. Announce the format, walk, read out and sit. This is not a local history tour. This is not a derive, I am not a flaneur, This is in-between those, informed by being a returnee to a city, as a sociologist. The themes of how we are un/building decompositions and recompositions runs through it. There is so much to see in the cathedral alone, we are only taking a particular route today.

- Group read when we step inside **Henri Lefebvre** before we go inside (Typo).

**Chapel of Unity** – glass panels given by Germany.

**People and Cities** - lecture read.

Follow the sound – head to **Chapel of Industry**.

Spend a bit of time with the text on the walls and the sound.

**Go to Ruins** – **Choir of Survivors** – mention **Noise of the Past**.

**Two Trees** by Yoko

Go Back to **Williams**

**11am. Live and Dye Garden** – Read **Kimmerer** x 2. Hear Greg et al.

Set off – some words about walking. Draw attention to **Solnit** \[me\]

**11.30am Herbert** wealthy tool manufacturer for engines, cars and aircraft . Benefactor. **Collect some notes** on what you see and how it connects with the Anthropocene.

My father worked as an **attendant** – childhood walks, constantly returning.

**One Mile Walk: Benjamin** - Ball Hill – Two tone village and the cathedral \[me\]

**Building Digging** – read

**Trees** – sit down – read in a circle, outside the Graham Sutherland.

**Car park** – Aya read

**Ring Road** – go to **medieval building** and read Nicolas

**Finish by 12 to bus stop**.
