# How to cite

There are several ways to link to and cite this book. Every citation follows the citation style you choose in the settings (Aa). Under **Copy citations as**, the settings also let you choose how citations are copied:

- **Formatted** (the preset) keeps the citation's font formatting. Use it when you paste into a word processor such as Word, Pages or Google Docs.
- **Plain text** copies the words alone, without italics or links. Use it when you want plain text.

Your citation settings for this book are saved in your browser.

## Cite a section

Each section (or page or chapter) starts with a short note at the top of its margin, or above the text on a phone. The note says who made the section and when. Its **Copy citation** button copies the citation for that section.

## Cite a quote

To cite a quote, select words on any page of the book and a **Copy quote** button appears at the foot of the window. It copies the quote, its citation and a link to the paragraph it comes from. The citation is numbered by paragraph, for example (Nassar 2026: para. 55). If you copy in the usual way (Cmd/Ctrl+C), you get only the words, without the citation.

## Cite a picture, recording, video or document

Every picture, recording, video and document in the book is a numbered item. The number can be found at the start of its caption.

- **Click the number** to open the item's page, where you find the item's citation, with a **Copy citation** button.
- **Alternatively, for images**, you can open the zoom viewer, where you'll find a **Cite** button that copies the citation of the picture or document you are viewing.

An item is cited as part of the book, with its own short address, for example (Puwar 2026: item 37) and hearhere.matteringpress.org/37/. Where the item's source has asked to be credited in a particular way, the citation includes that too.

## Cite a reference

All references are listed in the [Bibliography](/about/bibliography-and-items/#bibliography), in the About this book section. Under each work are the passages that cite it.

- **In the text**, click a citation such as (Hall 2001) to see the full reference. Its **Copy citation** button copies it.
- **In the Bibliography**, the copy icon ⧉ beside each work copies its reference.
- **For a reference manager**, the top of the Bibliography has links to download the whole bibliography as **BibTeX**, **RIS** or **CSL-JSON**.

Like every other copy on the site, references follow the citation style and the formatted or plain-text setting you choose in Aa.

## Link to a paragraph

Every paragraph has a number in the left margin. Point at the number and a ¶ appears beside it. Click it to copy a link straight to that paragraph.

## Citations and versions

The book is published in versions. Each version stays online at its own address, for example hearhere.matteringpress.org/v0.1/. Paragraph numbers and links of a published version do not change, so that citation links do not break.

Changes made between published versions are shown in a temporary working copy. When you cite a passage from the working copy that is new, or has changed since the last published version, the citation links to the working copy and says "unreleased". Its paragraph number may still change until the next version is published. If you want stable links, cite only published versions.

The versions are listed under [Licence and versions](#licence-and-versions) below.

## Citation style and reference managers

Citations follow Mattering Press' house style, which is based on MHRA. You can change to APA, Chicago, MLA or Harvard in the settings (Aa).
